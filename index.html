HOW TO PLAY
MOVE ONE STEP, CORNER TO CORNER. Forward, on the dark squares. Tap a piece to see where it can go.
JUMP OVER A PIECE TO TAKE IT. BONK! Hop over it onto the empty square behind it.
KEEP JUMPING: A DOUBLE JUMP! If you can jump again from where you land, keep going. It's all one move.
➜
REACH THE FAR SIDE: A KING! 👑 He gets a crown. Crowning ends your move.
KINGS GO BACKWARD TOO. A king moves and jumps both ways.
IF YOU CAN JUMP, YOU MUST! The pieces that must jump glow. Little ones can switch MUST JUMP off on the menu.
🏆=0
TAKE THEM ALL, OR BOX THEM IN! You win when they have no pieces left, or can't move.
🤝
40 MOVES EACH WITH NO JUMP AND NO NEW KING IS A DRAW. The count starts again after a jump or a crowning.
